# Klad — Product Overview

## What is Klad?

Klad is a thinking canvas for solo professionals and entrepreneurs. It's a space where you can dump your messy thoughts, ideas, plans, and notes — and then use AI to organize, analyze, and act on them.

Think of it like a whiteboard for your brain, but smarter.

---

## Current Features

### Core Canvas

- **Free-form drawing & note-taking** — Sticky notes, text boxes, shapes, and arrows
- **Organize your thinking** — Drag, group, and arrange your notes however you want
- **Multiple note types**:
  - Sticky notes (colored post-its)
  - Text nodes (plain text)
  - Rectangles and shapes (with optional text)
  - Hand-drawn freehand sketches
  - Arrows for connecting ideas
  - Image uploads and inline annotation

### Canvas Tools

- **Pan & Zoom** — Navigate your canvas smoothly, zoom in/out
- **Undo/Redo** — Full undo/redo stack for all changes
- **Select & Edit** — Select multiple notes, move them, resize them
- **Duplicate** — Clone notes quickly
- **Delete** — Remove notes you don't need
- **Styling** — Customize text size, color, stroke width, and fill

### AI-Powered Features

Klad's AI (powered by Claude) helps you work smarter with your notes:

#### 1. **Organize** — Group your notes intelligently
- "Group these by priority"
- "Organize by timeline"
- "Sort by complexity"
- The AI reads your notes and creates logical groups with visual organization on the canvas

#### 2. **Extract Tasks** — Turn notes into actionable to-dos
- "What are the tasks here?"
- "Give me next steps"
- The AI finds concrete action items hidden in your notes and creates a task board

#### 3. **Ask Questions** — Challenge your thinking
- "What assumptions might be wrong?"
- "What am I missing?"
- The AI asks sharp questions to poke holes in your logic and expose blind spots

#### 4. **Analyze** — Find patterns and insights
- "What are the main themes?"
- "What contradicts what?"
- The AI finds patterns, gaps, and relationships in your thinking

### Project Management

- **Multiple projects** — Create separate canvases for different ideas/initiatives
- **Free tier** — Up to 3 projects
- **Pro tier** — Unlimited projects + unlimited AI calls

### Authentication

- **Magic link sign-up** — No password needed, just your email
- **Account creation** — Automatic profile creation on signup

## Key Characteristics

### ✅ What Klad Does Well

- **Capture chaos** — No structure required, just write/draw freely
- **Organize thinking** — Turn mess into structure using AI
- **Challenge assumptions** — AI asks uncomfortable questions
- **Extract actions** — Turn vague ideas into concrete tasks
- **Work solo** — Designed for one person thinking, not collaboration
- **Preserve context** — Everything stays on your canvas for reference

### ❌ What Klad Doesn't Do

- **Team collaboration** — Not designed for shared projects
- **Answer general knowledge questions** — No weather, news, or trivia
- **Generate ideas from scratch** — Needs your input to work with
- **Write content** — Not a blog/document generator
- **Code or technical work** — Not for programming tasks

---

## User Profile

**Klad is for:**
- Solo founders and entrepreneurs
- Product managers planning features
- Strategists developing plans
- Anyone who thinks out loud and needs to organize their thinking
- People who want AI to help them think better, not think for them

**NOT for:**
- Teams or collaborative work
- People looking for quick answers
- Content creation workflows
- General-purpose AI chat

---

## How AI is Used

The AI in Klad is **reactive, not proactive**:
- It only works when you explicitly ask it to
- You select your notes and give it a task
- It analyzes what you've written and produces structured results
- You remain in control — the AI is a helper, not a decision-maker

The AI cannot:
- Access external information
- Generate ideas without your input
- Make changes without your request
- See notes you haven't shown it

---

## Current Limitations

- One user per canvas (no sharing or collaboration)
- Limited AI calls per month (free: 20, Pro: unlimited)
- Canvas is read-only when shared (view-only links exist but are limited)
- No mobile app yet
- No integrations with other tools

---

## Roadmap (Vision)

While this is the current state, the vision is to:
- Add smarter organization (auto-suggestions)
- Import/export (bring your data in and out)
- Better sharing options
- Mobile experience
- Integrations with task managers and calendars
- More AI capabilities (pattern detection, anomaly detection)

---

## Why Klad Exists

Most AI tools want to do the thinking for you. Klad is different — it's a tool for thinking better yourself. You bring the ideas, the thinking, the context. Klad helps you organize and challenge that thinking.

For solo professionals and entrepreneurs, clear thinking is a competitive advantage. Klad is designed to help you get there faster.
